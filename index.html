About Us

CV Royal Muda Berjaya

Founded in 2017, CV Royal Muda Berjaya — known by its brand Royal Estate Investment — has established itself as a reputable and reliable construction service provider based in Bali.

The company has consistently delivered high-quality projects, showcasing expertise and excellence across the construction industry. With a strong focus on innovation, efficiency, and customer satisfaction, every project meets the highest industry standards.

Whether it's a private residence, a high-end villa, or a government project, Royal Estate Investment continues to build excellence through strong leadership and a dedicated, skilled workforce.
